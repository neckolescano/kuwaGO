Why kuwaGO

Your phone keeps stealing your focus. So we gave it something to protect.

Most focus apps count minutes. kuwaGO gives those minutes something to look after: a small plant that grows when you stay and wilts when you leave.

Your guide is kuwago (Filipino for owl). It cheers you on, shows you around, and is a little sad when you give up.

How it works

Three steps. One tiny garden.

Set a focus time

Choose anywhere from 5 minutes to 2 hours. Your seed waits in the greenhouse.

Stay with it

Finish the session and your plant grows one stage. Give up and it wilts, but the next session perks it back up.

Fill your garden

Fully grown plants move into your garden, earn XP, and unlock new seeds to grow next.

AI study tools

Your notes, turned into practice.

Write in your Study Journal, then let kuwago make study material from it in one tap.

  • Quizzes with multiple choice, true/false and identification
  • Flashcards you flip and sort into Learned or Review again
  • Summaries, key ideas and friendly explanations
  • No internet? Simpler versions are made right on your phone
Your garden

Every plant you grow stays in your garden archive. Level up, keep your streak, and work toward the next seed.

  • XP, garden levels and a daily study streak

  • A study log of every session, synced across devices

Questions

Good to know.

Can't find your answer? Send a message.

Is this a subscription?

No. The Founding Gardener pass is a single ₱99 payment. Nothing renews and there's nothing to cancel.

Why pay now if it will be free on Google Play?

Publishing on Google Play costs money, and kuwaGO is made by one student. Founding Gardeners get the app months early, get every update first, keep their perks, and make the Play Store launch possible.

How long until I can install it?

Usually within a day after you email your receipt. The install invite comes from Firebase App Distribution, Google's tool for sharing apps with testers.

What if it doesn't work on my phone?

Message us. If we can't get kuwaGO running on your phone, you get a full refund to your GCash.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. kuwaGO is Android-only for now, so please don't buy the pass for an iPhone.

What happens to my notes and payment details?

Your notes are only sent to an AI service when you tap a study tool. Your GCash receipt is only used to confirm your payment and send your invite. Read the privacy policy for details.

Who makes kuwaGO?

kuwaGO is designed and built by Nicho Lescano, a student developer from the Philippines. It started as a class project and grew into a real app.
