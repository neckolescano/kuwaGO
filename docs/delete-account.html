kuwaGOPrivacy policy

Delete your kuwaGO account

kuwaGO by Nicho Lescano

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

  1. Open kuwaGO and go to Profile.
  2. Scroll down to Cozy settings and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Google accounts pick their account again; email accounts type their password.

Your account and all its data are deleted right away.

Option 2: by email (if you can't open the app)

Send an email to neckolescano36@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my kuwaGO account", from the email address you signed in with (or include it). We'll delete the account and confirm within 30 days.

Guest accounts have no email, so they can only be deleted from inside the app.

What gets deleted

  • Your login (Google or email account link with kuwaGO)
  • Your profile: gardener name and profile photo
  • Your garden: plants, harvests, XP, levels, badges, streaks and unlocks
  • Your notes and generated study material
  • Your study session log and settings

Nothing is kept afterwards. Deleted data may stay in Google's own encrypted backup systems for a short time before it is removed automatically.
