Delete your kuwaGO account

Two ways to remove your account and everything in it. Pick whichever works for you.

kuwaGO by Nicho Lescano
Option 1 · Fastest

In the app

  1. Open kuwaGO and go to Profile.
  2. Scroll down to Cozy settings and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Google accounts pick their account again; email accounts type their password.

Your account and all its data are deleted right away.

Option 2 · No app needed

By email

If you can't open the app, send an email to neckolescano36@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my kuwaGO account", from the email address you signed in with (or include it). We'll delete the account and confirm within 30 days.

Guest accounts have no email, so they can only be deleted from inside the app.

Write the email

What gets deleted

Your loginGoogle or email account link with kuwaGO
Your profileGardener name and profile photo
Your gardenPlants, harvests, XP, levels, badges, streaks and unlocks
Your notesAnd generated study material
Your historyStudy session log and settings

Nothing is kept afterwards. Deleted data may stay in Google's own encrypted backup systems for a short time before it is removed automatically.
