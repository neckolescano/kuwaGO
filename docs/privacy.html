kuwaGO Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

kuwaGO is a study app: a Pomodoro focus timer where finishing sessions grows pixel plants, with AI study tools made from your own notes. It is made by Nicho Lescano ("we"). This policy explains what data the app uses, why, and how you can delete it.

In short: we only keep what the app needs to save your garden and study progress. We don't show ads, we don't sell your data, and we don't use analytics or tracking tools. You can delete your account and all its data from inside the app at any time.

1. What we collect and why

DataWhy
Account: email address, display name, and a sign-in ID from Google Sign-In or email/password. Guest accounts have only an anonymous ID.To sign you in and keep your garden on your account across devices.
Profile: gardener name and an optional profile photo you choose.Shown on your Player Card. The photo is stored small and compressed.
Study data: your notes, generated questions and flashcards, plant and garden progress, XP, streaks, badges, settings, and a log of your study sessions (when, how long, finished or not).The core of the app: growing your garden, your Study Log and stats.
App integrity check (Firebase App Check with Google Play Integrity).To make sure requests come from the real kuwaGO app and protect the AI service from abuse.

Reminders and Deep Focus run on your phone only. Reminder times are stored on your device, and Deep Focus doesn't record which other apps you use.

2. AI features and your notes

When you tap Generate Study Material or use a Study Tool (Summary, Key Ideas, Explain), the text of your notes (and the topic you type) is sent to an AI service to create the result:

  • Google Gemini, through Firebase AI Logic (Google).
  • Groq, as a backup when Gemini is busy.

These services process your notes under their own terms and privacy policies (Google, Groq). Some free AI services may use submitted content to improve their products, so please don't put personal or sensitive information in your study notes. Nothing is sent to an AI unless you use one of these features. If no AI is available, a simpler version is made on your phone without sending anything.

3. Where your data is stored

Your account and study data are stored with Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore) on Google Cloud servers, with a copy on your phone so the app works offline. Database rules make sure each account can only read and write its own data.

4. Sharing

We don't sell or rent your data, and we don't share it for advertising. Data only goes to the service providers above (Google Firebase, Google Gemini, Groq) so the app can work, or where the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete everything at any time:

  • In the app: Profile → Cozy settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, garden, notes, study material, stats and study log right away.
  • Without the app: see Delete your kuwaGO account.

Signing out of a guest account also loses that garden, because there is no login to return to.

6. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and protected by Firebase security rules and App Check. No method is 100% secure, but we only keep what the app needs.

7. Children

kuwaGO is meant for students aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your choices

  • You can use a guest account without giving an email address.
  • You can change your gardener name and photo, or remove the photo, in Profile.
  • You can turn reminders off and choose not to use the AI features.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top and, for important changes, mention it in the app's update notes.
